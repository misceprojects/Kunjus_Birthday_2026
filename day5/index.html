Cover Story

Meet Nandana.

She is, by every available measure, one of the good ones. Kind-hearted to a fault, impossibly lovable, and the kind of person who remembers how you were feeling last week.

She also gets emotional at record speed. A sad reel, a kind word, a slightly sentimental song: tears. Scientists have stopped timing it.

But here's the thing. She's genuinely funny, and she chooses to be happy, even on the days that make it hard. That's rarer than it sounds.

Capable, stubborn, soft and strong all at once, she is the reason this magazine exists.

Investigation

Woman Refuses All Gifts, Cries on Contact

Mention a present in front of Nandana and you'll see it: the wobbly lip, the “no no no, why would you,” the tears arriving before the gift does.

Her stated reason? She believes nobody likes her enough to put in effort for her.

This publication has investigated that claim thoroughly. We have reviewed the evidence, consulted the experts and looked at the facts. Our conclusion: it is complete and total nonsense.

Editor's Note

You deserve effort. Lots of it. You're crazy to think otherwise. This entire magazine is effort, so deal with it.

Reader advisory: keep tissues within reach for the rest of this issue.

Dear Dr. Nandana,

If you've made it this far without crying, we failed. Hopefully we didn't.

Here's what this whole magazine is trying to say: you are loved. Properly, loudly, put-in-the-effort loved. You've worked so hard to get here, and you're kinder to everyone else than you are to yourself.

So be a little kinder to you. Accept the gifts. Take the compliments. Keep dancing, keep dressing up, keep being ridiculous, and keep that smile on.

Happy birthday, Doctor. This is only Volume One.

Kunjus,

I don't know how to start this without sounding dramatic, so I'll just say it: you're one of those people who quietly make life better just by being in it. You soften hard days and bring laughter where there wasn't any a moment ago.

And this year, you became a doctor. I watched you fight for it: the late-night textbooks, the endless exams, the days your brain was exhausted and your heart still refused to stop. Through all of it you stayed the same kind, joyful, slightly chaotic, always-laughing you. That's rare. Truly rare.

I hope this year brings you the kind of happiness that stays, like warm sunlight. I hope you're always surrounded by the people who have been your world. And I hope everyone finally sees what I've always seen: the quiet strength, the spark, the softness, the fire, the light.

Thank you for the laughter, the randomness, and the small moments that end up mattering most. I'm glad our paths crossed, and even happier that they stayed crossed.

So yeah… Happy Birthday, Kunjus 🤍
Keep going. Keep shining.
The world is better with you in it.

With love.
